e-conomic invoicing

A tool for accounting and bookkeeping firms, built in Google Sheets. It turns the monthly journey from employee timesheets to finished invoicing data into a matter of a few clicks.

  • Allocates hours to clients, annual accounts and internal work
  • Produces an invoice overview for review before invoicing
